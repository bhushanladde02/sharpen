Three steps, fifteen seconds each

Logging is the only thing we ask of you. Everything else — the score, the report, the profile — is built from it.

Log a session

Date, tool, task, minutes — then four honest questions: how much was yours, did you check it, did you learn, was it useful. The browser extension and usage exports fill in the volume for you.

Read your month

On the 1st you get a report: where the time went, how much of the thinking stayed human, and three concrete things to try next month. On screen and as a PDF.

Share the signal

Your public AI profile carries the score, the trend and the tools you use — nothing else. Companies compare candidates on how they work, not how much they prompt.
