Sharpen emails the same invitation, in your name, with a proper layout. The friend sees who it is from and gets one email — no reminders, no list.

Sharpen sends at most 10 a day for you, one per address, never to someone who already has an account or who asked not to be written to. The address is not kept — only a one-way hash, so those rules can be applied. Each email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. Privacy.

Sending from Sharpen is not switched on for this site, so the invitation goes from your own mail app or the share buttons — which is also where it is most likely to be read.
